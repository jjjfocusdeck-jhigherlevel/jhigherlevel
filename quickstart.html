The Protocol · First 24 Hours
Your only job todayMake the decisions below. Then use them before the day ends.
01

Choose One Outcome

Finish this sentence: “If only this one thing happens today, the day counts.” Pick the outcome that moves your life forward—not the easiest task on your list.

→ This is your Clarity pillar. One outcome. Two supporting tasks. One thing you refuse.
02

Protect Your Peak Window

Find the next 60–90 minutes when your mind is sharpest. Put it on your calendar now. Phone in another room. No meetings. No “quick checks.”

60–90 minutesOne taskPhone away
→ This is your Cadence pillar. Work with your biology, not the clock.
03

Name the Leak

Be surgical. Not “my phone.” Write the exact behavior, time, and trigger that steals your attention. You cannot contain what you have not named.

→ This is your Containment pillar. Change the environment before you ask for more willpower.
04

Set One Compounding Rep

Choose one small action that moves your most important goal forward. It must be small enough to do on your worst day. You are not proving intensity—you are building evidence.

→ This is your Compound pillar. Small daily reps. Reviewed weekly. Reset monthly.
05

Schedule the Weekly Review

Open your calendar and block 30 minutes this Friday. Title it: Focus Review — Non-Negotiable. A system without review becomes a forgotten document.

→ Review the wins, archive what is dead, and choose next week's one big thing.
